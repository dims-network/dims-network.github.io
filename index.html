Dynamic Interaction & Multimodal Signals

The DIMS-network

An open family of tools for multimodal social-interaction data — time series, video, transcripts and ELAN annotations on one timeline, beside the analyses that say how two signals relate: recurrence, cross-recurrence, cross-wavelet coherence and a cross-effector network. Coherence is read against a chance level estimated by simulation, because unrelated signals do not score 0 — a raw number cannot be interpreted without that comparison. One core; one small repository per study, each pinning a release of it.
